Accessibility Statement

Memory Movers NJ is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities.

Our Commitment

Memory Movers NJ is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to people with disabilities. We strive to meet or exceed the requirements of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, and applicable provisions of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

Conformance Status

We aim to conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This means we work to ensure our website is perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust for all users, including those who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, magnification software, and keyboard-only navigation.

Measures We Have Taken

  • Providing text alternatives for non-text content
  • Ensuring content is presented in a logical reading order
  • Using sufficient color contrast ratios
  • Making all functionality available from a keyboard
  • Providing visible focus indicators for interactive elements
  • Using proper heading hierarchy and semantic HTML landmarks
  • Including a "Skip to Main Content" link for keyboard users
  • Providing form labels and instructions
  • Ensuring interactive targets meet minimum size requirements
  • Supporting reduced motion preferences

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this website. If you encounter any accessibility barriers, please contact us:

  • Email: gwen@memorymoversnj.com
  • Phone: (201) 615-4956

We try to respond to accessibility feedback within 2 business days.

Compatibility

This website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

  • Screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver)
  • Screen magnification software
  • Speech recognition software
  • Keyboard-only navigation

Limitations

Despite our best efforts, some content may not yet be fully accessible. We are continually working to improve accessibility across our site. If you experience difficulty with any content, please contact us so we can provide the information in an alternative format.
